# Star Wars Rules & Lore Database

## Overview
This database contains comprehensive rules and lore synthesized from the major **Star Wars** themed tabletop games, including **Star Wars: Legion**, **Star Wars: X-Wing**, **Star Wars: Rebellion**, **Star Wars: Force and Destiny RPG**, and **Star Wars: Unlimited**. It provides a unified reference for the mechanics, factions, units, and lore of the Star Wars universe as represented in tabletop gaming.

## Database Structure
| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `combat-rules.json` | Core combat mechanics, turn sequences, and phase rules across all game types |
| `factions.json` | All major Star Wars factions with ideologies, strengths, and relationships |
| `units.json` | Iconic units, ships, and characters from the Star Wars universe |
| `scenarios.json` | Scenarios adapted from various Star Wars games and the broader saga |
| `lore.json` | Star Wars universe lore including the Force, eras, planets, and key events |
| `combat-tables.json` | Attack resolution tables for ground and space combat |
| `special-abilities.json` | Force powers, leader abilities, and unique unit capabilities |

## Key Mechanics (Synthesized)
- **Ground Combat (Legion)**: Alternating activations with command cards; units have wound thresholds, speed, weapons, and rank
- **Space Combat (X-Wing)**: Secret maneuver planning with dials, firing arcs, and dice-based attacks
- **Galaxy Control (Rebellion)**: Asymmetric 2-player game with hidden Rebel base, probe droids, and reputation track
- **Narrative Dice (RPG)**: Success/Failure with Advantage/Threat side effects; Triumph/Despair for major narrative effects
- **Card Game (Unlimited)**: Resource management, aspect system (6 aspects), alternating action phase, base HP system

## Factions
- **Galactic Republic / Jedi Order** — The democratic government and Force-wielding guardians of peace
- **Galactic Empire** — The authoritarian regime led by Emperor Palpatine and Darth Vader
- **Rebel Alliance** — The insurgency fighting to restore democracy and freedom
- **Separatist Alliance (CIS)** — The Confederacy of Independent Systems led by Count Dooku and General Grievous
- **Sith** — The dark side Force users seeking power and domination
- **The First Order / Resistance** — The sequel trilogy factions
- **Scum & Villainy** — Smugglers, bounty hunters, and criminals of the Outer Rim

## Core Concepts
- **The Force**: A mystical energy field binding the galaxy; Light Side vs Dark Side
- **Eras**: Old Republic, Prequel, Original Trilogy, Sequel, and High Republic
- **Planets**: Tatooine, Coruscant, Hoth, Endor, Naboo, Mustafar, and many more
- **Ships**: X-Wing, TIE Fighter, Millennium Falcon, Star Destroyer, Death Star

## Reception of Source Games
- **Legion**: Praised for alternating activations and capturing the chaos of blaster battles
- **X-Wing**: Beloved for its programming mechanic and tactical depth in space combat
- **Rebellion**: Acclaimed as one of the best asymmetric board games ever made
- **Force & Destiny**: Celebrated for narrative dice mechanics and rich Force power options
- **Unlimited**: Well-received for its aspect system and unlimited deckbuilding flexibility
