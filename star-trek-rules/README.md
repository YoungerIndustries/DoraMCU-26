# Star Trek Rules & Lore Database

## Overview
This database contains comprehensive rules and lore synthesized from the major **Star Trek** themed tabletop games, including **Star Trek Adventures (2d20)**, **Star Fleet Battles**, and **Star Trek: Alien Worlds**. It provides a unified reference for the mechanics, factions, units, and lore of the Star Trek universe as represented in tabletop gaming.

## Key Mechanics (Synthesized)
- **2d20 System (Star Trek Adventures)**: Roll 2d20 + Attribute + Skill vs Target Number. Results generate Successes, Advantages, Threats, and Complications using the 2d20 Outcome Table.
- **Action Dice**: When an action could cause harm or stress, roll d6 Action Dice to determine the degree of effect (Minor to Critical).
- **Space Combat (Star Fleet Battles)**: Hex-based movement with impulse-based turns. Ships fire phasers and torpedoes based on arc and range, with damage resolved on tables.
- **Stress and Damage**: Characters have two damage tracks — Stress (temporary) absorbs damage first, then Damage (permanent). When Damage equals Toughness, the character is incapacitated.
- **Ship Combat (RPG)**: Starship combat uses the 2d20 system with bridge officers' skills modifying ship capabilities. Ships have Stress and Damage tracks similar to characters.

## Factions
- **Starfleet (United Federation of Planets)** — The exploratory and defensive service of the Federation
- **Klingon Empire** — A militaristic empire that values honor and warrior culture
- **Romulan Star Empire** — A secretive empire that survives through deception and strategic superiority
- **Cardassian Union** — An expansionist empire built on order, discipline, and conquest
- **The Dominion** — A powerful empire controlled by shapeshifting Founders from the Gamma Quadrant
- **The Borg Collective** — A hive mind seeking to assimilate all sentient life
- **Bajor** — A small world that resisted Cardassian occupation and plays a pivotal role in Alpha Quadrant politics
- **The Maquis** — A paramilitary group fighting against perceived Federation betrayal

## Core Concepts
- **The Prime Directive**: Starfleet's General Order 1 forbids interference with pre-warp civilizations
- **Eras**: TOS (2260s), TNG (2360s), DS9 (2369-2375), VOY (2371-2378), Kelvin Timeline (2258+)
- **Species**: Humans, Vulcans, Klingons, Romulans, Borg, Changelings, Trill, Betazoids, and many more
- **Starships**: Enterprise (all variants), Voyager, Defiant, Klingon D7, Romulan Warbird, Borg Cube
- **Technology**: Warp drive, transporters, phasers, photon torpedoes, cloaking devices, holodecks, tricorders

## Reception of Source Games
- **Star Trek Adventures**: Praised for its faithful adaptation of all Star Trek eras and the elegant 2d20 narrative system
- **Star Fleet Battles**: Beloved for its deep tactical space combat simulation and massive scale engagements
- **Star Trek: Alien Worlds**: Celebrated for its exploration-focused gameplay and first contact mechanics
