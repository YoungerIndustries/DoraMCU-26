# Lord of the Rings Galaxy — Design Guide

A comprehensive reference for creating a space-faring galaxy inspired by J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium.

---

## 1. Cosmology Overview

### The Universe: Eä
In Tolkien's mythology, **Eä** is the name for the entire created universe — "the thing that is." It is the Music of the Ainur made manifest by Eru Ilúvatar. In a galaxy setting, Eä can represent the entire Milky Way or a larger cosmic structure.

### The World: Arda
**Arda** is the Earth/world within Eä. Tolkien later equated Arda with the Solar System, where Valinor exists on another planet and the Sun and Moon are celestial objects. For a galaxy, Arda can be the name of the galaxy itself or a central star cluster.

### The Veil of the Stars
Tolkien described the stars as existing in **Tar-menel** (the upper airs) immediately above Arda, between **Vista** and **Ekkaia**. In a galaxy setting, this can be reimagined as a dimensional boundary or a nebula that separates the known galaxy from the Timeless Halls beyond.

## 2. Celestial Bodies

### The Sun: Anar (Vása)
- **Guardian**: Arien (Maiar)
- Created from the light of Laurelin (the Golden Tree)
- In a galaxy: a bright G-type star at the center of a key system

### The Moon: Isil / Ithil (Rána)
- **Guardian**: Tilion (Maiar)
- Created from the light of Telperion (the Silver Tree)
- Tilion was said to be in love with Arien
- In a galaxy: a large moon or a dim white dwarf companion star

### The Star of Eärendil
- Eärendil and his ship Vingilot, bearing a Silmaril, were placed in the sky as a sign of hope
- This is the brightest "star" visible from Middle-earth
- In a galaxy: a unique beacon — a luminous anomaly or a ship-sized artifact visible across light-years, serving as a navigation point

## 3. Constellations (Star Clusters / Systems)

| **Remmirath** | Netted Stars | Pleiades | A dense star cluster; a trade nexus or mining region |
| **Soronúmë** | Eagle of the West | Aquila or Lyra | A western frontier system; surveillance or scouting outpost |
| **Telumendil** | Lover of the Stars | Boötes (possibly) | A mysterious or isolated system |
| **Wilwarin** | Butterfly | Cassiopeia (possibly) | A beautiful, symmetrical system; elven cultural center |
| **Anarríma** | Sun Border | Gemini (possibly) | A system near the galactic core or a bright nebula |

## 4. The Valar — Planets Named After the Gods

The Valar are the god-like beings who shaped Arda. A popular fan concept (used in Stellaris mods) is to create a star system where each planet is named after a Vala, reflecting their domain:

### Lords of the Valar

| Vala | Domain | Planet Concept |
|---|---|---|
| **Manwë Súlimo** | King of the Valar; Lord of Air and Wind | A gas giant with powerful atmospheric storms; the capital world |
| **Ulmo** | Lord of Waters; King of the Sea | An ocean world with no landmasses; covered in deep seas |
| **Aulë** | Smith and master of crafts; father of the Dwarves | A heavily industrialized mining world; rich in metals and mithril |
| **Oromë** | Great Hunter; Lord of Forests | A forest-covered world with vast wilderness and wildlife |
| **Mandos (Námo)** | Judge; Doomsman; Lord of Fate | A somber, ancient world; a prison or archive planet |
| **Irmo (Lórien)** | Master of Visions and Dreams | A mysterious world with psychic or dream-like properties |
| **Tulkas Astaldo** | Champion; greatest in strength | A war world or military fortress planet |

### Queens of the Valar (Valier)

| Vala | Domain | Planet Concept |
|---|---|---|
| **Varda Elentári** | Queen of the Stars; kindler of light | A luminous world; a star-forging or energy-producing planet |
| **Yavanna Kementári** | Giver of Fruits; Queen of the Earth | A lush agricultural world; the breadbasket of the galaxy |
| **Nienna** | Lady of Pity and Mourning | A quiet, contemplative world; a healing or sanctuary planet |
| **Estë** | The Gentle; healer of weariness | A restorative world; a resort or recovery station |

## 5. Middle-earth Regions as Star Systems

Each major region of Middle-earth can become a star system or sector:

### The Shire
- **Type**: Peaceful, agrarian system
- **Description**: A quiet, green system on the galactic fringe — overlooked by major powers but strategically located
- **Inhabitants**: Hobbits — small, unassuming but resilient colonists

### Rivendell (Imladris)
- **Type**: Ancient elven sanctuary
- **Description**: A hidden system protected by spatial distortions; an archive of ancient knowledge
- **Inhabitants**: Elves led by Elrond; scholars and diplomats

### Lothlórien
- **Type**: Golden, timeless realm
- **Description**: A system bathed in golden light; time flows differently here
- **Inhabitants**: Elves led by Galadriel and Celeborn

### Moria (Khazad-dûm)
- **Type**: Underground dwarf stronghold
- **Description**: A hollowed-out planet or asteroid belt with vast underground forges; rich in mithril
- **Inhabitants**: Dwarves of the Seven Houses; led by the line of Durin

### Rohan
- **Type**: Nomadic horse-lord territory
- **Description**: A system of open plains and grasslands; fast ships and cavalry culture
- **Inhabitants**: Men of Rohan; allied with Gondor

### Gondor
- **Type**: Ancient Númenórean kingdom
- **Description**: A powerful, centralized empire with white towers and advanced palantír (communication) technology
- **Capital**: Minas Tirith — a great fortress world
- **Inhabitants**: Númenórean descendants; stewards and rangers

### Mordor
- **Type**: Dark, volcanic empire
- **Description**: A scorched system dominated by a red star; industrial war machine
- **Capital**: Barad-dûr — a dark fortress station orbiting a lava world
- **Inhabitants**: Orcs, trolls, and corrupted beings under Sauron

### Isengard
- **Type**: Industrialized fortress
- **Description**: A world stripped of nature; massive factories and war machines
- **Ruler**: Saruman the White — a fallen wizard seeking power through technology

### Númenor (Elenna)
- **Type**: Lost island empire
- **Description**: A once-great system that was destroyed or sunk into a black hole; its ruins are legendary
- **Legacy**: The Starwards people; their descendants founded Gondor

### Harad
- **Type**: Southern exotic lands
- **Description**: Hot, tropical systems with unique flora and fauna; trade partners or rivals
- **Inhabitants**: Southrons — diverse cultures

### Rhûn
- **Type**: Eastern steppes
- **Description**: Vast, open systems on the eastern rim; nomadic and warlike
- **Inhabitants**: Easterlings

### Forodwaith
- **Type**: Northern ice wastes
- **Description**: Frozen systems at the galactic pole; dangerous and sparsely populated
- **Notable**: The northern lights (aurora) are visible here

### Mirkwood
- **Type**: Dark forest system
- **Description**: A system shrouded in dark energy or nebula; dangerous to navigate
- **Inhabitants**: Wood-elves, spiders, and other creatures

### Fangorn
- **Type**: Ancient forest world
- **Description**: A living world where the trees themselves are sentient (Ents)
- **Inhabitants**: Treebeard and the Ents — ancient, slow-moving guardians

## 6. The Rings of Power — Galactic Artifacts

The Rings can be reimagined as powerful galactic artifacts or technologies:

### The One Ring
- **Type**: Ultimate control device
- **Function**: A device that can command all other Rings; grants immense power but corrupts the user
- **Location**: Lost in the fires of Mount Doom (a volcanic star or planet core)

### The Three Elven Rings
- **Narya** (Fire): A weapon or energy source; held by Gandalf
- **Nenya** (Water): A shield or preservation device; held by Galadriel
- **Vilya** (Air): The most powerful of the Three; held by Elrond

### The Seven Dwarf Rings
- Granted wealth and enhanced crafting ability but led to their corruption (dragon-sickness)

### The Nine Human Rings
- Created the Nazgûl — nine wraith-ships or cursed warlords bound to Sauron

## 8. Factions and Powers

### Free Peoples Alliance
- **Gondor** — The central human empire
- **Rohan** — Cavalry allies
- **Rivendell** — Elven diplomatic center
- **Lothlórien** — Elven spiritual center
- **The Shire** — Neutral but strategically important
- **Moria** — Dwarven industrial power
- **The Istari** — Advisors and agents

### Forces of Darkness
- **Mordor** — Sauron's war empire
- **Isengard** — Saruman's industrial war machine (sometimes independent)
- **Harad & Rhûn** — Vassal states and allies of Mordor
- **The Nazgûl** — Nine wraith-commanders

### Neutral / Independent
- **Blue Wizards** — Operating in the East and South
- **Ents** — Ancient guardians of Fangorn
- **Beornings and other outlaws** — Independent operators

## 9. Key Concepts for Galaxy Design

### The Palantírs
Reimagined as a galactic communication network — seven seeing-stones that allow instant communication across light-years. Controlled by Gondor, but some fell into enemy hands.

### The Silmarils
Three jewels containing the light of the Two Trees — the most precious artifacts in the galaxy. One is in the sky (with Eärendil), one is in the sea, and one is in the earth.

### The Two Trees
- **Telperion** (Silver) — Source of the Moon's light
- **Laurelin** (Gold) — Source of the Sun's light
Their destroyed remnants can be ancient energy sources or sacred sites.

### Valinor (The Undying Lands)
A hidden realm beyond the Veil of the Stars — a protected sector or dimension where the Valar dwell. Only those invited may enter. In a galaxy, this could be a walled garden behind a spatial barrier.

### The Doors of Night
The boundary between the mortal galaxy and the Undying Lands — a one-way gateway or a black hole event horizon.

### Sindarin (Grey Elven) — for common, everyday names
- Examples: Imladris (Rivendell), Minas Tirith, Mordor, Eregion

### Dwarven (Khuzdûl) — for dwarf-related names
- Examples: Khazad-dûm (Moria), Erebor, Durin, Moria

### Adûnaic (Númenórean) — for human empire names
- Examples: Arnor, Gondor, Númenor, Anárion

                              |

- **Valacirca** is the central hub — the seven-star system where major trade routes converge
- **West** (Rivendell, Shire) = Safe, elven-influenced systems
- **East** (Mordor, Rhûn) = Dangerous, war-torn systems
- **South** (Harad) = Exotic, trade-oriented systems
- **North** (Forodwaith) = Frozen, isolated systems

## 13. Species and Races

| Race | Galaxy Role | Traits |
|---|---|---|
| **Elves** | Ancient, wise diplomats and scholars | Immortal, attuned to the stars, fading from the galaxy |
| **Men** | Rising power; explorers and builders | Mortal but ambitious; gifted with the desire to explore beyond the stars |
| **Dwarves** | Master engineers and miners | Stubborn, resilient, excel at underground and asteroid operations |
| **Hobbits** | Unassuming but resilient | Overlooked by major powers; surprisingly tough and adaptable |
| **Ents** | Ancient guardians of forest worlds | Extremely slow to act but devastating when roused |
| **Orcs** | War machines of Mordor | Mass-produced soldiers; corrupted beings |
| **Maiar** | Powerful spiritual beings | Include the Wizards, Balrogs, and nature spirits |
