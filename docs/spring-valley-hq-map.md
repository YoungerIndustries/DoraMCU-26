# Younger Industries HQ — Spring Valley, CA 91977 Map

## Overview

**Younger Industries (YISY)** headquarters is located in **Spring Valley, California 91977**, an unincorporated census-designated place in the East County region of San Diego County. The community has a population of approximately 32,000–33,000 residents and is characterized by dense suburban development, hilly terrain, residential neighborhoods, and commercial corridors along major roads.

**Coordinates:** Spring Valley is situated east of San Diego city proper, near La Mesa and Lemon Grove.

**Geographic Boundaries:**
- **North:** State Route 94 (Highway 94) — major east-west freeway
- **West:** State Route 125 — north-south freeway to San Diego
- **East:** Sweetwater River corridor and San Diego National Wildlife Refuge
- **South:** Bonita and Jamul communities

### Terrain
Spring Valley features **hilly terrain** with dense suburban development. The Sweetwater River runs through the eastern portion, creating a natural boundary with the San Diego National Wildlife Refuge. The area is prone to wildfires, with multiple fire stations in the region (Potrero Fire Station #31, Ramona Fire Station #81 and #82).

### History
The area was originally part of the Mexican land grant "Rancho Jamacha." Campo Road and Highway 94 historically brought commerce through the Jamacha Valley. The valley was a popular weekend destination for travelers from San Diego. Two rail terminals served neighboring Spring Valley — one through Lemon Grove to the San Diego waterfront, and another to National City. Regular stage service on Campo Road operated from 1870 through 1912.
