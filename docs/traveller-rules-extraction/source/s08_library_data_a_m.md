# S08: Library Data (A-M) — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Supplement 8: Library Data (A-M) (1980/1981), GDW

## Contents

### The Imperium (Page 7)
- Over 1100 worlds in a region ~700 parsecs across
- 11 centuries of rule, expanding
- Two solutions to distance problem: Xboat Network and Feudalism
- **Xboat Network**: Jump-4 courier ships, 44 weeks edge-to-core (50% faster than normal)
- **Feudalism**: Sectors (~32x40 parsecs), subsectors (8x10 parsecs), dukes rule subsectors
- Honor-based system cultivated by hereditary aristocracy

### Interstellar Society (Page 8)
- 100+ intelligent species in Imperium, 400+ total known
- **Major Races**: Achieved FTL independently (Aslan, Centaurs, Droyne, Hivers, Humaniti, Vargr)
- **Minor Races**: Did not achieve FTL independently
- **Six Races**: Aslan, Centaurs, Droyne, Hivers, Humans, Vargr
- Psionics: Strong in Humaniti, requires training; Zhodani train nobles only; Imperial population generally anti-psionics

### The Six Races (Detailed)
- **Aslan**: Lion-like, 2m tall, 100kg, carnivore/pouncer stock, warrior race, clan-based society
- **Centaurs (K'kree)**: 1.5m shoulder, 550kg, herbivore stock, claustrophobic, gregarious, caste system
- **Droyne**: 1m tall, winged herbivore stock, 6 castes (Worker, Warrior, Drone, Technician, Sport, Leader)
- **Hivers**: 1.5m tall, 150kg, omnivore gatherer/scavenger, six-fold radial symmetry, highly individualistic
- **Humaniti**: Solomani (Terra), Vilani (Vland), Zhodani (Zhodane) as major human races
- **Vargr**: Genetically manipulated from canine stock by Ancients, prone to fighting, racial pride

### Library Data Entries (A-M)

#### Vehicles
- **Airlraft**: 4t, anti-grav, 100kph, open-topped, weather-vulnerable, orbit-capable
- **All Terrain Vehicle (ATV)**: 10t, 6t payload, amphibious, pressurized, 8 passengers, 100kph roads/60kph cross-country/20kph broken

#### Travel Zones
- **Amber Zone**: Caution — chaos, upheaval, xenophobia
- **Red Zone**: Interdicted — no entry (TAS classification)

#### Key Locations
- **Ancients**: Extinct race, ~300,000 BP, destroyed themselves in 2,000-year war, created asteroid belts, disseminated Humaniti, created Vargr
- **Andory**: Prime Droyne homeworld candidate, interdicted since 802
- **Capital**: Imperial capital, central hub
- **Kirur**: Centaur homeworld, capital of Two Thousand Worlds
- **Kuzu**: Aslan homeworld, capital of Aslan Hierate
- **Lair**: Vargr "homeworld", capital of Vargr Extents
- **Glea**: Hive coordinating center, Glean Cluster
- **Guaran**: Hiver homeworld

#### Naval Concepts
- **Battle Rider**: Non-jump combat ship carried by tender, ton-for-ton superior to battleship
- **Battleship**: Jump-capable combat ship, can flee if outmatched
- **Battle Tender**: Jump-capable transport for battle riders, dispersed structure
- **Gunboat**: 200-600t, vacuum/atmosphere/water capable, system defense
- **Cruiser**: Independent operations, supports main battle line

#### Historical Events
- **Civil War (604-622)**: 18 Emperors of the Flag, 7 assassinated, 10 killed in battle, 1 survived (Arbellatra)
- **First Frontier War (589-604)**: Imperium vs Outworld Coalition (Zhodani+Vargr)
- **Solomani Rim War (990-1002)**: Imperium vs Solomani Confederation, Terra captured, armistice
- **Third Frontier War (979-986)**: Imperium vs Zhodani
- **Fourth Frontier War (1082-1084)**: "False War", short and inconclusive

#### Imperial Concepts
- **Imperial Edict 97**: Enabling act for Imperial warrants — assist warrant holder with all available power
- **Imperial Warrant**: Bypasses bureaucracy, issued by Emperor for specific missions
- **Imperial Research**: Black holes, instant transmitters, gravity manipulation, genetics, AI, psionics, robotics, time travel
- **Interdiction**: World placed under travel ban, requires Imperial family approval

#### Megacorporations (Page 40)
- **Delgado Trading, LIC**: Military hardware, mining, publishing, founded 997
- **General Products, LIC**: Heavy industry, starships, founded 620-622
- **GSbAG**: Starships only, Imperial family vessels, high quality/high price
- **Hortalez et Cie, LIC**: Banking, investment, insurance, mercenary clearinghouse
- **Instellarms, LIC**: Mercenary arms trade, founded 626
- **Ling-Standard Products**: Mining, electronics, vehicles, starships
- **Makhidkarun**: Communications, entertainment, gourmet foods (pre-Third Imperium)
- **Naasirka**: Information processing, computers, robots (pre-Sylean)
- **SuSAG, LIC**: Chemicals, pharmaceuticals, geneering, anagathics, psi drugs
- **Sharurshid**: Luxury goods trade, rare Terran wines
- **Sternmetal Horizons, LIC**: Mining, power generation, food synthesis
- **Tukera Lines, LIC**: Passenger and freight shipping monopoly
- **Zirunkariish**: Banking, investment, insurance (Vilani, founded -425)

#### Titles (Page 40)
- **LIC**: Limited liability Imperial Charter (required for interstellar commerce)
- Archaic titles: Ltd, Inc, Amt, AG, SA (charm or pre-Imperium status)
- Vilani titles: Makhidkarun, Naasirka, Sharurshid, Zirunkariish (predate Third Imperium)

#### The Solomani (Page 36)
- **Solomani People**: From Terra, achieved spaceflight ~2500 years before Third Imperium
- **Solomani Movement**: Pure Terran racial stock superiority, peaked mid-600s, lost favor after Zhakirov married Antiama (679)
- **Solomani Autonomous Region (704-940)**: 50 parsecs around Terra, self-rule
- **Solomani Rim War (990-1002)**: Imperial attempt to reabsorb, Terra captured, armistice

### Astrography (Page 17)
- Coordinate system: rings of longitude, rays of latitude, parsecs
- Format: xxxx ring/ray yyyyy
- Example: Regina = 9930 ring/ray 62723
- 10,000th ring = baseline through Capital
- Effective for ~400 parsecs wide band at longitude 10,000

### Dating Systems (Page 24)
- **Terran**: BC/AD, 365 days, centered on mid-Vilani period
- **Vilani**: PI/VI, 1.33 standard years, from First Imperium founding
- **Imperial**: Year 0 = holiday year, Julian day numbering (001-1105)
- **Zhodani**: Olympiads (3 Zhodani years = 2.25 standard years)

### Galactic Directions (Page 24)
- **Coreward**: Toward galactic core (also toward Capital)
- **Rimward**: Away from galactic core
- **Spinward**: Direction of galactic rotation
- **Trailing**: Opposite of spinward
