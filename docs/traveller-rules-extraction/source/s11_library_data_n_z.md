# S11: Library Data (N-Z) — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Supplement 11: Library Data (N-Z) (1982), GDW

## Contents

### Imperial Space (Page 6)
- Imperium rules space between stars, not worlds themselves
- Worlds self-govern if they pay taxes, acknowledge Imperial power, obey basic laws
- Imperial power in space: consulates, bureaucratic offices, bases
- Mapping: sectors → subsectors → systems; domains above sectors

### The Domains (Page 6)
- Origin: Pacification campaigns, Emperor Artemsus divided map into 6 areas
- **Sylea**: Reserved for Emperor (4 sectors)
- **Vland**: Named after Vilani homeworld
- **Gateway**: —
- **Ilelish**: Former Rule of Man territory, easily absorbed
- **Antares**: Partially absorbed, not part of Rule of Man
- **Sol**: —
- **Deneb**: Created 589 (First Frontier War), archduke never appointed (Civil War)
- **Current Status**: Little practical significance, no taxes, no navy bureaucracy level
- **Archduke Powers**: Create knights/baronets, hereditary lands/income, Emperor surrogates

### The Sectors (Page 7)
- 16 subsectors per sector
- Rigid geometrical mapping system
- Adjustments for real universe (e.g., Reft split by Great Rift, Corridor administered through Vland)

### Library Data Entries (N-Z)

#### Naval Bases (Page 11)
- **Naval Base**: Port facility for naval vessels, orbital (1000t+) and surface (<1000t)
- Parts: Orbital berthing, surface berthing, maintenance, administrative HQ
- Not a tactical unit, no battle forces
- **Naval Depot**: System-wide focus, construction/repair, experimental prototypes
- Peacetime: Design and testing; Wartime: Repair and resupply
- Tight security, entry/exit codes, marines and defense boats
- Usually one per sector

#### Nth Interstellar War (Page 12)
- Indeterminate number of wars between Terran Confederation and First Imperium (-2408 to -2219)
- First War (-2408 to -2400): Initial clash
- Wars 2-7: Seesaw exchanges, Dingir/Sol subsectors only
- Eighth War: First major Terran victory
- Ninth War: Terran jump-3 drive, crushing victory, Solomani Rim lost
- Nth War (-2235 to -2219): Final war, Vilani Imperium collapses

#### Olympiad (Page 12)
- Zhodani time measure: 3 Zhodani years (2.25 standard years)
- Marks period between psionic games

#### Outworld Coalition (Page 13)
- Zhodani + Vargr alliance against Imperium
- First Frontier War: Vargr portions failed, coalition collapsed
- Reformed after 5-year hiatus (Civil War weakness)
- Second Frontier War: Took portions of Jewell subsector
- Sword Worlds also joined at times

#### Pacification Campaigns (76-120) (Page 13)
- Economic, diplomatic, military operations to force membership
- Predominantly peaceful, force when necessary
- Ended 120, initial phase of Imperium growth complete

#### Phoenix Project (Page 13)
- Solomani guerrilla plot on Terra to destroy Imperial forces
- Prepositioned caches of military goods
- Families entrusted with cache locations
- Imperial counter-guerrilla effort 1040-1045
- Lambda (trigger codeword) and Omega (off-world reinforcement) — dismissed as baseless
- Many historians believe extent was exaggerated

#### Psionic Games (Page 15)
- Zhodani competition, once per olympiad, ~4 weeks on Zhodane
- Participants: Mostly intendants, selected by ruling councils
- Success = promotion to noble status
- Events: Telekinetic weight-lifting, distance/accuracy teleportation, etc.

#### Vegan Autonomous District (Page 31)
- Created after Solomani Rim War as counterweight to Solomani
- Complete internal control, free passage for Imperial citizens
- Centralized government, civil service by competitive examination

#### Vilani (Page 31)
- Human major race from Vland
- First jump drive: -9235
- Rigid caste system, cultural rigidity led to stagnation
- Currently cultural label, not racial/national
- Language survives in some places, music and fine arts fragments
- Old Vilani noble families control Vilani megacorporations

#### Vland (Page 31)
- Vilani homeworld, former First Imperium capital
- Current Vland sector capital, major trade/manufacturing center

#### Imperial Rules of War (Page 31)
- Unwritten, case-by-case concepts
- Not codified to prevent precedent blocking intervention
- Main aim: Maintain economic and military well-being
- Tolerates force as political/social pressure outlet
- Extra-planetary seizure of world affairs = beyond scope

### The Imperial Nobility (Page 34)

#### Noble Ranks (Page 35)
- **Baronet**: Lowest rank, hereditary, creates no vassals
- **Knight**: Hereditary, may hold fief
- **Baron**: Subsector-level noble
- **Viscount**: Multiple subsectors
- **Count**: Sector-level noble
- **Earl**: Multiple sectors
- **Marquess**: Domain-level noble
- **Duke**: Major domain noble
- **Archduke**: Domain ruler (6 archdukes)
- **Emperor**: Supreme ruler

#### Noble Powers
- Create lower nobles (subject to Emperor disapproval)
- Hereditary lands and incomes
- Surrogates for Emperor at functions

### History of the Spinward Marches (Page 38)

#### The Frontier Wars (Page 39)
- **First Frontier War (589-604)**: Outworld Coalition vs Imperium, Olav hault-Plankwell victorious, sparked Civil War
- **Second Frontier War (615-620)**: Reformed Coalition, took Jewell portions, Arbellatra victorious
- **Third Frontier War (979-986)**: Mismanaged, led to Styryx abdication
- **Fourth Frontier War (1082-1084)**: "False War", short and inconclusive, Battle of Two Suns
- **Fifth Frontier War (1107-present)**: Latest, Zhodani attacks with guerrilla uprisings

#### Astrography (Page 41)
- Spinward Marches sector coordinates
- Map of sector with subsectors

### Politics in the Solomani Rim (Page 44)
- Solomani Confederation vs Third Imperium
- Ongoing tension, rare border incidents
- Solomani propaganda about Terra liberation
- Real Solomani attention on rimward frontier

#### Astrography (Page 45)
- Solomani Rim sector coordinates
- Map of sector with 16 subsectors

### Zhodani Consulate
- Psionic-based government
- Intendants: Psionic-trained administrators
- Nobles: All trained in psionics
- Rival of Imperium on spinward border
- Outworld Coalition leader
