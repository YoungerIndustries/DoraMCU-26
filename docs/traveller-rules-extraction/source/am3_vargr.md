# AM3: Vargr — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Alien Module 3: Vargr (1984), GDW

## Contents

### Overview
- The Vargr Extents is one of the Six Races, a major power bordering the Third Imperium
- Vargr are wolf-like humanoids, genetically created by the Ancients
- Capital: Lair, the Vargr "homeworld"
- Population: ~50 billion across ~500 worlds
- Known for their pack culture and nomadic raiding

### Biology
- **Appearance**: Wolf-like humanoids, 1.5-2 meters tall, ~80 kg
- **Anatomy**: Digitigrade legs, tail, fur-covered body, canine head with fangs
- **Senses**: Excellent night vision, acute hearing, exceptional sense of smell
- **Diet**: Carnivorous, require meat protein
- **Lifespan**: ~60 standard years (shorter than humans)
- **Reproduction**: Gestation ~5 months, litters of 2-4 offspring
- **Physical Traits**: 
  - +1 DM to melee combat (fangs and claws)
  - Enhanced pack tactics bonus in combat
  - Natural stealth in forest or jungle terrain
  - Howling communication over long distances

### Society
- **Pack System**: Society organized around packs (extended families)
  - Each pack has an alpha (leader)
  - Pack loyalty is absolute
  - Inter-pack rivalry is common
- **Extents Government**: 
  - The Extents is ruled by the Alpha Prime, elected from among pack alphas
  - The Alpha Prime serves for life
  - Council of Alphas advises the Alpha Prime
- **Lair**: The "homeworld" (actually an Ancient creation site)
  - Political center of the Extents
  - Sacred to Vargr religion

### Culture
- **Pack Bond**: Pack membership is everything
  - Betrayal of pack is the ultimate sin
  - Pack members share all resources
  - Exile from pack is a fate worse than death
- **Nomadic Nature**: Vargr are restless wanderers
  - Frequent migration between systems
  - Raiding is a cultural tradition
  - Settlement is rare and temporary
- **Warrior Ethos**: Combat is central to Vargr life
  - Raiding skills are taught from puppyhood
  - Honor is won through combat prowess
  - Peace is seen as weakness
- **Social Structure**:
  - Alphas (pack leaders)
  - Warriors (raiders and hunters)
  - Hunters (scouts and foragers)
  - Workers (craftsmen and caregivers)
  - Outcasts (exiled from pack)

### Character Generation (Vargr)

#### Block
- **ST**: 3D6 (normal strength)
- **DX**: 3D6+1 (enhanced reflexes)
- **ED**: 3D6+1 (emotional intensity)
- **IN**: 3D6-1 (pack thinking over individual)
- **ML**: 3D6+1 (strong will)
- **BA**: 3D6

#### Skills
- **Combat**: Melee Weapons (fangs), Firearms, Pack Tactics
- **Movement**: Running, Stealth (in natural terrain), Tracking
- **Social**: Leadership (for alphas), Intimidation
- **Survival**: Hunting, Navigation (natural sense of direction)

#### Career Restrictions
- Vargr may serve in any career, but:
  - Military careers are preferred
  - Naval careers are common (raiding tradition)
  - Civilian careers are rare

### The Vargr Extents
- **Government**: Extents (elective monarchy)
- **Population**: ~50 billion
- **Worlds**: ~500
- **TL**: 10-12
- **Economy**: Raid-based, plunder and tribute
- **Military**: Large raiding fleets, guerrilla tactics
- **Relations with Imperium**: Hostile, frequent raiding
  - First Frontier War: 589-604 (Vargr + Zhodani vs Imperium)
  - Current status: Cold war, constant border raids

### Vargr Traits (Game Mechanics)
- **Combat DM**: +1 melee (fangs and claws)
- **Social DM**: -1 with non-Vargr (xenophobia)
- **Navigation DM**: +1 (natural sense of direction)
- **Armor Bonus**: 0
- **Trade Bonus**: 0%
- **Special**: Pack tactics bonus in combat, enhanced night vision, howling communication

### Vargr Language
- **Primary**: Vargr (howls, growls, barks)
- **Secondary**: Trade tongue (Imperial) for interstellar commerce
- **Writing**: Scratch marks, pictographic

### Vargr Ships
- **Design Philosophy**: Fast, agile, heavily armed for raiding
- **Common Types**: 
  - Raiding ships (fast, heavily armed)
  - Transport vessels (troop movement)
  - Patrol ships (border security)
- **Notable**: Vargr ships are smaller and faster than Imperial equivalents, designed for hit-and-run tactics
