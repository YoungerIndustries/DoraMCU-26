# S02: Animal Encounters — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Supplement 2: Animal Encounters (1979), Metagaming

## Contents

### Overview
- Pre-generated animal encounter tables for major terrain types
- Special vacuum encounter rules for space animals
- Animal statistics including movement, attack, and damage
- Reaction tables for animal encounters
- Survival tips for different terrain types

### Terrain Types and Encounter Tables

#### Vacuum (Space)
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per week in space
- **Animals**: Vacuum worms, space mites, void jellyfish
- **Special rules**: Animals can damage ship hulls and sensors
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Desert
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day on surface
- **Animals**: Sand worms, desert lizards, scorpions, flying serpents
- **Hazards**: Sandstorms, extreme heat, dehydration
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Grassland
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day on surface
- **Animals**: Herbivores, predators, insects, birds
- **Hazards**: Stampedes, predation, disease
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Water
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day in water
- **Animals**: Aquatic predators, fish, cephalopods, reptiles
- **Hazards**: Drowning, currents, underwater predators
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Urban
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-12 per day (lower density)
- **Animals**: Rats, insects, domesticated animals, mutants
- **Hazards**: Disease, feral animals, pollution effects
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Underground
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day underground
- **Animals**: Blind creatures, insects, burrowers, cave dwellers
- **Hazards**: Collapses, darkness, toxic gases
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Jungle
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-4 per day (higher density)
- **Animals**: Predators, insects, reptiles, amphibians, birds
- **Hazards**: Disease, venom, terrain navigation
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Hills
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day on surface
- **Animals**: Mountain goats, birds of prey, reptiles, insects
- **Hazards**: Falls, rockslides, exposure
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Mountains
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-8 per day (lower density)
- **Animals**: Mountain predators, birds, insects, cave dwellers
- **Hazards**: Altitude sickness, falls, cold
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Ice
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day on surface
- **Animals**: Arctic predators, fish, birds, insects
- **Hazards**: Cold, hypothermia, thin ice
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Neutral
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day on surface
- **Animals**: Generic fauna based on world TL
- **Hazards**: Variable based on specific world
- **Table size**: 12 entries

#### Gas Giant
- **Encounter chance**: 1-in-6 per day in atmosphere
- **Animals**: Flying creatures, atmospheric drifters
- **Hazards**: Pressure changes, storms, gas toxicity
- **Table size**: 12 entries

### Animal Statistics

Each animal has:
- **Name**: Common name of the creature
- **Movement**: Ground/air/water speed in km/h
- **Attack**: Number of attacks per round
- **Damage**: Damage dice per attack
- **Armor**: Natural armor class (0-8)
- **Size**: Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, Massive
- **Reaction**: Friendly, Neutral, Hostile, Territorial

### Reaction Table (d6)
| Roll | Reaction | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Friendly | Animal approaches curiously, may be tamed |
| 2-3 | Neutral | Animal observes but keeps distance |
| 4-5 | Hostile | Animal attacks on sight |
| 6 | Territorial | Animal defends territory, may chase intruders |

### Special Rules

#### Vacuum Animals
- Can survive in space without protection
- May damage ship hulls (1D6 MV damage per hit)
- Can clog intakes and damage sensors
- Require special weapons to kill in vacuum

#### Giant Animals
- Some worlds have giant versions of normal animals
- Size modifier: +1D6 damage per size category above normal
- Movement may be slower but more powerful

#### Venomous Animals
- Some animals inject venom on attack
- Venom causes additional damage over time
- Antidote may be available at starports

#### Herd Behavior
- Some animals travel in herds/packs
- Herd size: 2D6 × 10 individuals
- Pack tactics: +1 DM to attack in groups

### Survival Tips by Terrain

#### Desert
- Carry water purification tablets
- Travel at night to avoid heat
- Watch for sandstorms on horizon

#### Jungle
- Use insect repellent
- Watch for venomous creatures
- Mark trail to avoid getting lost

#### Water
- Know swimming abilities before entering
- Watch for aquatic predators
- Avoid murky water

#### Underground
- Bring light sources
- Watch for collapses
- Test air quality

### Encounter Resolution

1. **Determine terrain type** from world UWP
2. **Roll on appropriate table** for animal type
3. **Roll reaction** to determine initial attitude
4. **Resolve combat** if hostile
5. **Award experience** for survival

### Experience Awards
- **Survival**: 1D6 × 100 credits per successful encounter
- **Hunting**: Animal value based on size and rarity
- **Discovery**: New species = 5D6 × 100 credits
