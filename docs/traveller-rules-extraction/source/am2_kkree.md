# AM2: K'kree (Centaurs) — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Alien Module 2: K'kree (1984), GDW

## Contents

### Overview
- The Centaur Two Thousand Worlds is one of the Six Races
- K'kree (Centaurs) are horse-like humanoids, massive and powerful
- Capital: Kirur, the Centaur homeworld
- Population: ~50 billion across ~2000 worlds
- Known for their claustrophobia and love of open spaces

### Biology
- **Appearance**: Horse-like upper body, human-like lower body, 1.5m at shoulder, ~550 kg
- **Anatomy**: Four legs, massive torso, powerful neck, equine head
- **Senses**: Excellent distance vision, good hearing, poor close-range manipulation
- **Diet**: Herbivorous, require large quantities of plant matter
- **Lifespan**: ~100 standard years
- **Reproduction**: Gestation ~12 months, single offspring
- **Physical Traits**: 
  - Massive strength (ST+2)
  - Claustrophobic — suffer penalties in confined spaces
  - Excellent runners
  - Poor fine motor skills

### Society
- **Caste System**: Society organized around hereditary castes
  - **Nobles**: Ruling class, political leaders
  - **Warriors**: Military class, border defense
  - **Merchants**: Trade and commerce
  - **Artisans**: Craftsmen and builders
  - **Laborers**: Agricultural and industrial workers
- **Two Thousand Worlds Government**: 
  - Ruled by the High Council of Nobles
  - Each world sends representatives
  - Consensus-based decision making (slow but thorough)
- **Kirur**: The sacred homeworld
  - Site of the High Council
  - Religious center of the Two Thousand Worlds

### Culture
- **Gregarious Nature**: Centaurs are highly social
  - Loneliness is considered a punishment
  - Group activities are preferred
- **Open Spaces**: Strong preference for open terrain
  - Confined spaces cause anxiety
  - Ship design reflects this (large, open decks)
- **Honor and Duty**: Strong sense of obligation
  - Promises are kept
  - Duty to caste and society
- **Art and Music**: Rich cultural traditions
  - Poetry, music, and dance are highly valued
  - Oral history is important

### Character Generation (K'kree)

#### Block
- **ST**: 3D6+2 (massive strength)
- **DX**: 3D6-1 (poor fine motor skills)
- **ED**: 3D6+1 (emotional stability)
- **IN**: 3D6
- **ML**: 3D6+1 (strong will)
- **BA**: 3D6-1 (size causes issues)

#### Skills
- **Combat**: Firearms (two-handed), Riding
- **Movement**: Running, Swimming
- **Social**: Leadership (for nobles), Diplomacy
- **Craft**: Artisan skills (caste-dependent)

#### Career Restrictions
- K'kree may serve in any career, but:
  - Naval careers are difficult (claustrophobia)
  - Military careers are common
  - Trade careers are preferred by merchant caste

### The Two Thousand Worlds
- **Government**: Council of Nobles
- **Population**: ~50 billion
- **Worlds**: ~2000
- **TL**: 11-13
- **Economy**: Agricultural and resource-based
- **Military**: Large ground forces, limited navy
- **Relations with Imperium**: Neutral, trade partners
  - No major conflicts
  - Trade agreements in place

### K'kree Traits (Game Mechanics)
- **Combat DM**: 0
- **Social DM**: +1 (gregarious nature)
- **Navigation DM**: -1 (claustrophobia)
- **Armor Bonus**: +1 (massive size)
- **Trade Bonus**: +5% (merchant caste)
- **Special**: Claustrophobia penalty in confined spaces, massive strength

### K'kree Language
- **Primary**: K'kree (staccato, rhythmic)
- **Secondary**: Trade tongue (Imperial) for interstellar commerce
- **Writing**: Hoof-print based, pictographic

### K'kree Ships
- **Design Philosophy**: Large, open decks, minimal confinement
- **Common Types**: 
  - Merchant vessels (large cargo capacity)
  - Transport ships (troop movement)
  - Patrol vessels (border defense)
- **Notable**: Centaur ships are larger than Imperial equivalents, with open deck plans
