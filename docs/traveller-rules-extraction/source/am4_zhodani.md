# AM4: Zhodani — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Alien Module 4: Zhodani (1985), GDW

## Contents

### Overview
- The Zhodani Consulate is one of the Six Races, a major power bordering the Third Imperium
- Zhodani are human-like with natural telepathic abilities
- Capital: Zhodane, the Zhodani homeworld
- Population: ~50 billion across ~500 worlds
- Known for their psionic government and collectivist society

### Biology
- **Appearance**: Human-like, slight differences in bone structure and brain chemistry
- **Anatomy**: Enhanced neural pathways for psionic ability
- **Senses**: Normal human senses plus psionic perception
- **Diet**: Omnivorous, similar to humans
- **Lifespan**: ~90 standard years (longer than humans due to psionic training)
- **Reproduction**: Similar to humans
- **Physical Traits**: 
  - Natural psionic ability (telepathy, empathy)
  - Psionic ability varies by individual
  - Training enhances natural ability

### Society
- **Psionic Government**: Society organized around psionic ability
  - The Consulate is ruled by the Consuls, elected from among the psionic elite
  - The Consuls serve for life
  - Council of Psionics advises the Consuls
- **Zhodane**: The sacred homeworld
  - Site of the Consuls' court
  - Religious and political center of the Consulate
  - Interdicted by Zhodani law — non-Zhodani may not land

### Culture
- **Collectivism**: Individual identity is secondary to the collective
  - Personal desires are subordinated to the group
  - Dissent is seen as mental illness
  - Conformity is valued
- **Psionic Training**: All Zhodani receive psionic training
  - Training begins at age 6
  - Those without ability are considered defective
  - Psionic ability determines social status
- **Anti-Imperial**: Strong opposition to the Third Imperium
  - The Imperium suppressed psionics
  - Frontier Wars are seen as defensive
  - Imperial citizens are viewed with suspicion

### Character Generation (Zhodani)

#### Block
- **ST**: 3D6 (normal strength)
- **DX**: 3D6 (normal dexterity)
- **ED**: 3D6+1 (emotional control from psionic training)
- **IN**: 3D6+1 (enhanced intelligence from psionic training)
- **ML**: 3D6+2 (strong will from psionic training)
- **BA**: 3D6

#### Skills
- **Psionic**: Telepathy, Empathy, Mind Reading
- **Combat**: Firearms, Stealth
- **Social**: Diplomacy, Deception
- **Scientific**: Psychology, Psionics

#### Career Restrictions
- Zhodani may serve in any career, but:
  - Psionic careers are preferred
  - Military careers are common (Frontier Wars)
  - Civilian careers are rare

### The Zhodani Consulate
- **Government**: Consulate (psionic oligarchy)
- **Population**: ~50 billion
- **Worlds**: ~500
- **TL**: 12-14
- **Economy**: Centralized planning, psionic efficiency
- **Military**: Psionic warfare, stealth tactics
- **Relations with Imperium**: Hostile, Frontier Wars
  - First Frontier War: 589-604 (Zhodani + Vargr vs Imperium)
  - Third Frontier War: 979-986
  - Current status: Cold war, espionage

### Zhodani Traits (Game Mechanics)
- **Combat DM**: 0
- **Social DM**: +1 (empathy and telepathy)
- **Navigation DM**: 0
- **Armor Bonus**: 0
- **Trade Bonus**: 0%
- **Special**: Natural psionic ability, enhanced intelligence and will

### Zhodani Language
- **Primary**: Zhodani (similar to Imperial, with psionic components)
- **Secondary**: Trade tongue (Imperial) for interstellar commerce
- **Psionic**: Telepathic communication (no words, pure thought)

### Zhodani Ships
- **Design Philosophy**: Stealth, psionic warfare, electronic countermeasures
- **Common Types**: 
  - Stealth ships (undetectable by sensors)
  - Psionic warfare vessels (mind attack capabilities)
  - Espionage ships (intelligence gathering)
- **Notable**: Zhodani ships are designed for stealth and electronic warfare, not direct combat
