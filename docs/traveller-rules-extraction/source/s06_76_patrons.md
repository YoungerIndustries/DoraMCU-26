# S06: 76 Patrons — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Supplement 6: 76 Patrons (1980), GDW
Design: Loren K. Wiseman

## Contents

### Overview
S06 provides 76 non-player character patrons who can present missions of various difficulty for player characters. Contains:
- **60 patron encounter situations** for role-playing adventures
- **16 mercenary tickets** for military-style missions
- Each patron situation has 3+ resolution options (200+ total combinations)
- Designed for groups from single adventurers to dozen+ players

### Patron Encounter System

#### Structure
Each patron encounter includes:
1. **Patron Description**: Who the patron is and their background
2. **Mission Briefing**: What the patron wants done
3. **Complications**: Unexpected challenges and obstacles
4. **Resolutions**: 3+ possible outcomes with rewards and consequences
5. **Hooks**: Connections to other adventures or ongoing plots

#### Patron Types
- **Government Officials**: Imperial, planetary, or sector authorities
- **Corporate Representatives**: Megacorporation agents and executives
- **Criminal Elements**: Gang leaders, smugglers, and pirates
- **Personal Contacts**: Friends, family, and acquaintances
- **Religious Figures**: Clerics and spiritual leaders
- **Alien Representatives**: Non-human patrons and ambassadors

#### Mission Difficulty
- **Easy**: Low risk, modest reward, suitable for low-level characters
- **Medium**: Moderate risk and reward, standard missions
- **Hard**: High risk, substantial reward, requires resources and planning
- **Extreme**: Very high risk, potentially lethal, major rewards

### Patron Encounters (Selected Examples)

#### Government Patrons

**1. Imperial Consul**
- **Mission**: Investigate trade violations on a frontier world
- **Complications**: Local governor is corrupt; evidence is hidden
- **Resolutions**: Expose corruption, negotiate settlement, or cover up
- **Reward**: 10,000-50,000 Cr and Imperial favor

**2. Planetary Governor**
- **Mission**: Secure resources for planetary defense
- **Complications**: Resources are on a hostile world
- **Resolutions**: Diplomatic acquisition, theft, or military action
- **Reward**: 20,000-100,000 Cr and land grant

**3. Naval Commander**
- **Mission**: Deliver classified documents to allied system
- **Complications**: Pursued by enemy forces
- **Resolutions**: Successful delivery, evasion, or combat
- **Reward**: 30,000-150,000 Cr and naval commission

#### Corporate Patrons

**4. Megacorporation Executive**

- **Complications**: Corporate espionage and counter-espionage
- **Resolutions**: Successful sabotage, exposure, or double-cross

**5. Shipping Magnate**
- **Mission**: Escort valuable cargo through dangerous space
- **Complications**: Pirates and hostile forces
- **Resolutions**: Safe delivery, partial loss, or total loss

### Mercenary Tickets

#### Overview
16 mercenary mission tickets for security, commando, cadre, and striker forces. Each ticket includes:
- **Mission Type**: Security, assault, reconnaissance, etc.
- **Force Required**: Number and type of troops
- **Duration**: Expected mission length
- **Pay**: Compensation per trooper
- **Hazards**: Expected casualties and risks

#### Ticket Types

**Security Tickets**
- Protect VIPs, facilities, or convoys
- Lower risk, steady pay
- Examples: Embassy guard, mining camp security

**Commando Tickets**
- Direct action and raids
- Medium risk, higher pay
- Examples: Sabotage, hostage rescue

**Cadre Tickets**
- Training and advisory roles
- Lower risk, long-term commitment
- Examples: Train local forces, military advisory

**Striker Tickets**
- Elite special operations
- Highest risk, highest pay
- Examples: Deep reconnaissance, assassination

### Resolution System

#### Resolution Options
Each patron encounter provides 3+ resolution paths:
1. **Optimal Resolution**: Best outcome, full reward
2. **Partial Resolution**: Mixed outcome, reduced reward
3. **Failed Resolution**: Negative outcome, possible penalties
4. **Alternative Resolution**: Unexpected outcome, variable rewards

#### Reward Types
- **Monetary**: Credits and cash payments
- **Material**: Ships, equipment, property
- **Political**: Favor, influence, positions
- **Information**: Secrets, intelligence, data
- **Reputation**: Fame, notoriety, respect

#### Consequence System
- **Positive Consequences**: Allies, resources, opportunities
- **Negative Consequences**: Enemies, debts, legal trouble
- **Long-term Consequences**: Ongoing plot hooks and complications

### Campaign Integration

#### Quick Reference
- Patrons can be used as drop-in adventures
- No extensive preparation required
- Suitable for one-shots or campaign integration

#### Scaling
- Missions can be scaled for character level
- Rewards adjusted for difficulty
- Complications added for experienced groups

#### Cross-References
- Patrons may reference other adventures
- Ongoing plots can connect multiple patrons
- Factions and organizations appear in multiple encounters
