# AM5: Droyne — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Alien Module 5: Droyne (1985), GDW

## Contents

### Overview
- The Droyne are one of the Six Races, a minor power within the Third Imperium
- Droyne are small, winged humanoids with six castes
- Capital: Andory (interdicted, exact location unknown)
- Population: ~10 billion across ~100 worlds
- Known for their caste system and artistic culture

### Biology
- **Appearance**: Small winged humanoids, 1 meter tall, ~20 kg
- **Anatomy**: Wings (4-6 meters span), lightweight bones, herbivore digestive system
- **Senses**: Excellent distance vision, good hearing, poor sense of smell
- **Diet**: Herbivorous, require plant matter
- **Lifespan**: ~120 standard years
- **Reproduction**: Gestation ~8 months, single offspring
- **Physical Traits**: 
  - Natural flight (atmospheric only)
  - Lightweight build (ST-1)
  - Enhanced balance and coordination
  - Poor strength

### Society
- **Caste System**: Society organized around 6 hereditary castes
  - **Worker Caste**: Agricultural and industrial labor
  - **Warrior Caste**: Military and security
  - **Drone Caste**: Service and maintenance
  - **Technician Caste**: Science and engineering
  - **Sport Caste**: Art, entertainment, and athletics
  - **Leader Caste**: Political and religious leadership
- **Government**: 
  - Ruled by the Leader caste
  - Council of Leaders makes decisions
  - Caste membership is hereditary and immutable
- **Andory**: The sacred homeworld
  - Interdicted since 802 (Imperial decree)
  - Exact location unknown
  - Religious center of the Droyne

### Culture
- **Artistic Nature**: Droyne are highly artistic
  - Music, dance, and visual arts are central to life
  - The Sport caste is highly respected
  - Beauty is valued above all
- **Caste Loyalty**: Caste identity is paramount
  - Inter-caste marriage is forbidden
  - Caste determines occupation and social status
  - Mobility between castes is impossible
- **Peaceful Disposition**: Droyne prefer diplomacy over conflict
  - Warrior caste is small
  - Trade and culture are preferred
  - Military action is a last resort

#### Skills
- **Combat**: Firearms (light weapons only)
- **Movement**: Flight (atmospheric), Balance
- **Social**: Diplomacy, Art
- **Craft**: Artisan skills (caste-dependent)

#### Career Restrictions
- Droyne may serve in any career, but:
  - Military careers are rare (peaceful nature)
  - Artistic careers are preferred
  - Trade careers are common

### The Droyne
- **Government**: Caste oligarchy
- **Population**: ~10 billion
- **Worlds**: ~100
- **TL**: 12-14
- **Economy**: Art and culture-based, luxury goods
- **Military**: Small, defensive only
- **Relations with Imperium**: Neutral, trade partners
  - Andory interdicted since 802
  - Trade agreements in place
