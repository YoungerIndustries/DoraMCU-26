# AM6: Solomani — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Alien Module 6: Solomani (1986), GDW

## Contents

### Overview
- The Solomani Confederation is a major power within the Third Imperium
- Solomani are humans from Terra (Earth), with a distinct cultural identity
- Capital: Terra (Earth), the Solomani homeworld
- Population: ~100 billion across ~500 worlds
- Known for their political intrigue and Terran supremacy ideology

### Biology
- **Appearance**: Human, identical to other humans
- **Anatomy**: Standard human anatomy
- **Senses**: Normal human senses
- **Diet**: Omnivorous, similar to other humans
- **Lifespan**: ~80 standard years
- **Reproduction**: Similar to other humans
- **Physical Traits**: 
  - No physical differences from other humans
  - Cultural identity is the distinguishing factor
  - Solomani consider themselves the "true" humans

### Society
- **Political System**: Society organized around political factions
  - The Confederation is ruled by the High Council, elected from among political leaders
  - The High Council serves for fixed terms
  - Political parties compete for power
- **Terra**: The sacred homeworld
  - Site of the High Council
  - Religious and political center of the Confederation
  - Captured by the Imperium during the Solomani Rim War (990-1002)

### Culture
- **Terran Supremacy**: Solomani believe in Terran racial superiority
  - The Solomani Hypothesis posits Terra as the original homeworld of all humans
  - Solomani see themselves as the purest descendants of the Ancients
  - Other humans are viewed with suspicion
- **Political Intrigue**: Politics is central to Solomani life
  - Factionalism is common
  - Espionage and sabotage are accepted tools
  - Loyalty shifts with political winds
- **Anti-Imperial**: Strong opposition to the Third Imperium
  - The Imperium is seen as an illegitimate government
  - The Solomani Rim War was an attempt to regain independence
  - Current status: Military rule, underground resistance

- **ED**: 3D6 (normal education)
- **IN**: 3D6+1 (enhanced intelligence from political culture)

- **BA**: 3D6

#### Skills
- **Political**: Diplomacy, Deception, Administration
- **Combat**: Firearms, Stealth
- **Social**: Leadership, Intimidation
- **Scientific**: History, Politics

#### Career Restrictions
- Solomani may serve in any career, but:
  - Political careers are preferred
  - Military careers are common (resistance movement)
  - Civilian careers are rare

### The Solomani Confederation
- **Government**: Confederation (elective republic)
- **Population**: ~100 billion
- **Worlds**: ~500
- **TL**: 12-14
- **Economy**: Centralized planning, resource-based
- **Military**: Large standing army, resistance movement
- **Relations with Imperium**: Hostile, underground resistance
  - Solomani Rim War: 990-1002 (Solomani vs Imperium)
  - Current status: Military rule, underground resistance
