# B09: Pirates — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Book 9: Pirates (2025), Mongoose Publishing
Design: Rob Eaglestone
48-56 pages, digest-sized softcover (Little Black Book format)

## Overview
Book 9 is the first new Classic Traveller Book release in decades, providing comprehensive rules and tools for pirate campaigns. It expands on the Pirate career from S04 with advanced character generation, privateering mechanics, corsair starship designs, and a ready-to-play border subsector.

### Contents
1. Advanced pirate character generation system
2. The Practice of Piracy — economics, background, and game rules
3. Ship searching and boarding rules
4. Imperial response to pirate attacks
5. Corsair starship designs and acquisition rules
6. Privateering mechanics and economics
7. Border subsector for pirate campaigns

---

## Pirate Character Generation

### Enlistment
- **Enlistment Roll**: 7+ (modified by characteristics)
- **Modifiers**: +1 for low Social Standing (SS 7-), +1 for high Endurance
- **Terms**: 4 years each
- **Description**: Organized space piracy operating outside Imperial law with paramilitary structure

### Basic Training
- First year of service includes basic training
- Characters assigned to troops section or crew positions
- Training covers combat, ship operations, and pirate customs

### Survival
- **Survival Roll**: 8+ (DM +1 if In 8+)
- Higher risk than most careers due to dangerous nature of piracy
- Privateering missions carry additional risk

### Position and Promotion
- Pirates have a promotion/advancement system
- Advancement from crew member to officer to captain
- Promotion rolls determine rank progression

### Skills
Pirate character generation includes skills not found in core Classic Traveller rules, all referenced in one-page summary:

- **Gun Combat**: Handgun, SMG, Rifle, Shotgun, Carbine, Laser Weapons
- **Blade Combat**: Dagger, and other melee weapons
- **Pilot**: Space and ship's boat piloting
- **Navigation**: Starship navigation
- **Steward**: Passenger and crew services
- **Gunnery**: Ship-mounted weapons operation
- **Ship's Tactics**: Naval combat tactics
- **Recruiting**: Crew acquisition and management
- **Gambling**: Games of chance
- **Electronics**: Electronic systems
- **Carousing**: Social skills in taverns and bars
- **Forward Observer**: Artillery spotting
- **Brawling**: Unarmed combat
- **Leader**: Command and leadership

### Retirement
- Retirement points accumulated during service
- Retirement benefits include credits, weapons, and possibly ships
- **Ship Acquisition**: Retiring pirates may receive a ship (e.g., Type P Corsair)
- Credits vary based on service and rank

## The Practice of Piracy

### Economics of Piracy
- Pirate operations driven by profit from plunder
- Loot distribution follows pirate codes and hierarchy
- Successful raids fund further operations
- Privateering offers legal cover for piracy under letters of marque

### Background
- Pirates operate in frontier systems and border regions
- Imperial Navy response is the primary threat
- Local systems may have their own defense forces
- Timing is critical: pirates must push their luck against response times

### Game Rules
- Pirate attacks trigger response from local and Imperial forces
- Response time varies by system and distance to naval bases
- Successful pirates balance aggression with escape planning
- Privateering provides legal protection when operating under warrant

## Ship Searching and Boarding

### Searching Ships
- Rules for determining what is valuable on captured ships
- Cargo assessment and valuation
- Passenger ransom potential
- Ship itself may be captured as prize

### Boarding Actions
- Boarding procedures for capturing vessels
- Crew combat during boarding
- Securing captured vessel
- **Critical Rule**: Any character with Engineering skill and 45 minutes can fill a ship with fuel and blow it up
- Countermeasures and alarms available to bridge crew
- Disgruntled engineers represent a nightmare threat

---

## Imperial Response

### Response Mechanics
- Local systems may have defense forces
- Imperial Navy response time based on distance
- Response varies by system importance and naval presence
- Timing is essential: pirates must complete raids and escape before response arrives

### Response Types
- **Local Defense**: System-based forces, fastest response
- **Imperial Navy**: Regular naval units, slower response
- **Mercenary Forces**: Hired protection, variable response
- **Civilian Response**: Minimal combat capability
