# S04: Citizens of the Imperium — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Supplement 4: Citizens of the Imperium (1979), GDW
Design: Marc W. Miller

## Contents

### Overview
S04 provides 12 new career types for character generation, greatly expanding the options beyond the core military careers. Each career includes generation tables and 40 pre-generated NPC examples. The supplement makes the "Other" career from the core rules largely obsolete.

### New Career Types

#### 1. Pirate
- **Enlistment**: 7+ (DM +1 if SS 7-)
- **Terms**: 4 years
- **Survival**: 8+ (DM +1 if In 8+)
- **Position/Promotion**: Yes
- **Description**: Organized space piracy, operating outside Imperial law
- **Skills**: Gun Combat, Pilot, Navigation, Steward
- **Notes**: Paramilitary structure with advancement system

#### 2. Belter
- **Enlistment**: 8+ (DM +2 if In 6+)
- **Survival**: 9+ (DM = terms served)
- **Position/Promotion**: No
- **Skills per term**: 2 (no advancement system)
- **Description**: Asteroid belt miners and workers
- **Skills**: Mechanic, Pilot (ship's boat), Vacc Suit
- **Notes**: Hazardous work environment, high mortality

#### 3. Sailor
- **Enlistment**: 6+
- **Survival**: 7+
- **Position/Promotion**: Yes
- **Description**: Merchant marine and naval sailors
- **Skills**: Pilot, Navigation, Mechanic, Steward
- **Notes**: Civilian maritime career

#### 4. Diplomat
- **Enlistment**: 9+ (DM +1 if In 8+, DM +1 if SS 8+)
- **Survival**: Auto
- **Position/Promotion**: Yes
- **Skills per term**: 2
- **Description**: Imperial diplomatic service
- **Skills**: Diplomat, Legal, Administration, Broker
- **Notes**: Two skills per term, no advancement

#### 5. Doctor
- **Enlistment**: 9+ (DM +1 if In 8+)
- **Survival**: Auto
- **Position/Promotion**: No
- **Skills per term**: 2 (no advancement system)
- **Description**: Medical professionals and healers
- **Skills**: Medic, Surgery, Psychology, Science (Life)
- **Notes**: Two skills per term, no advancement

#### 6. Flyer
- **Enlistment**: 7+ (DM +1 if Dx 8+)
- **Survival**: 7+
- **Position/Promotion**: Yes
- **Description**: Atmospheric pilots and aviators
- **Skills**: Pilot (air/raft), Pilot (ship's boat), Navigation
- **Notes**: Focus on atmospheric flight

#### 7. Barbarian
- **Enlistment**: 6+ (DM +1 if St 8+)
- **Survival**: 8+
- **Position/Promotion**: No
- **Skills per term**: 2 (no advancement system)
- **Description**: Low-technology society warriors
- **Skills**: Melee Combat, Survival, Hunter
- **Notes**: For characters from pre-industrial worlds

#### 8. Bureaucrat
- **Enlistment**: 8+ (DM +1 if In 8+)
- **Survival**: Auto
- **Position/Promotion**: Yes
- **Description**: Imperial civil service
- **Skills**: Administration, Legal, Diplomat, Broker
- **Notes**: Rarely chosen by players but useful for NPCs

#### 9. Rogue
- **Enlistment**: 6+ (DM +1 if SS 8-, DM +2 if En 7+)
- **Survival**: Auto
- **Position/Promotion**: No
- **Skills per term**: 2 (no advancement system)
- **Description**: Criminals, thieves, and con artists
- **Skills**: Streetwise, Gambling, Deception, Locksmith
- **Notes**: Two skills per term, no advancement

#### 10. Noble
- **Enlistment**: No roll (requires SS 10+)
- **Survival**: Auto
- **Position/Promotion**: Yes
- **Description**: Imperial nobility and aristocracy
- **Skills**: Administration, Diplomat, Legal, Steward
- **Notes**: High social standing required

#### 11. Scientist
- **Enlistment**: 6+ (DM +1 if In 8+)
- **Survival**: Auto
- **Position/Promotion**: No
- **Skills per term**: 2 (no advancement system)
- **Description**: Research scientists and scholars
- **Skills**: Science (various), Administration, Legal
- **Notes**: Two skills per term, no advancement

#### 12. Hunter
- **Enlistment**: 7+ (DM +1 if Dx 8+)
- **Survival**: 8+
- **Position/Promotion**: No
- **Skills per term**: 2 (no advancement system)
- **Description**: Big game hunters and trackers
- **Skills**: Gun Combat, Survival, Tracking, Medicine
- **Notes**: Two skills per term, no advancement

### Career Categories

#### Careers with Advancement (Position/Promotion)
- Pirate, Sailor, Diplomat, Flyer, Bureaucrat, Noble
- Use standard position and promotion tables
- More skill rolls through advancement

#### Careers without Advancement
- Belter, Doctor, Rogue, Scientist, Hunter
- Two skills per term automatically
- No position or promotion rolls
- Simpler generation process

### Pre-Generated NPCs

#### NPC Examples
- 40 pre-generated NPCs for each career type
- Total: 480 pre-generated characters
- Include full UPP and skill lists
- Designed for use as NPCs or player character templates

#### Notable NPCs
- 8 heroes and villains from well-known stories included as examples
- NPCs grouped by career type for easy reference
- Time-saver material for referees

### New Skills Introduced

#### Combat Skills
- **Hunter**: Tracking and hunting techniques
- **Streetwise**: Urban survival and criminal knowledge

#### Social Skills
- **Diplomat**: Negotiation and protocol
- **Deception**: Lying and concealment
- **Gambling**: Games of chance and skill

#### Technical Skills
- **Locksmith**: Opening and setting locks
- **Administration**: Office and management skills

### Campaign Integration

#### Player Characters
- Expanded character options beyond military careers
- Civilian and criminal backgrounds available
- More diverse party compositions possible

#### Non-Player Characters
- Large pool of pre-generated NPCs
- Quick reference for populating campaigns
- Examples for each career type

#### World Building
- Careers reflect different world types and cultures
- Low-TL worlds have Barbarians and Hunters
- High-TL worlds have Scientists and Bureaucrats
- Frontier worlds have Belters and Pirates

### Design Notes

#### Skill Bloat Concern
- Introduction of new skills paved way for skill proliferation
- Core Traveller valued relatively spare skill list
- New skills undermined simplicity to some degree
- Easy to ignore most new skills and focus on careers

#### Career Balance
- Some careers more attractive than others
- Pirate and Rogue popular for roleplaying
- Bureaucrat rarely chosen by players
- All careers useful for NPC generation
