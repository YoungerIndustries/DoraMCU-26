# AM1: Aslan — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Alien Module 1: Aslan (1984), GDW

## Contents

### Overview
- The Aslan Hierate is one of the Six Races, a major power bordering the Third Imperium
- Aslan are lion-like humanoids, descended from carnivore/pouncer stock
- Capital: Kuzu, the Aslan homeworld
- Population: ~100 billion across ~1000 worlds

### Biology
- **Appearance**: Lion-like humanoids, 2 meters tall, ~100 kg
- **Anatomy**: Digitigrade legs, tail for balance, retractable claws, fangs
- **Senses**: Enhanced night vision, acute hearing, excellent sense of smell
- **Diet**: Carnivorous, require meat protein
- **Lifespan**: ~80 standard years
- **Reproduction**: Gestation ~6 months, litters of 1-3 offspring
- **Physical Traits**: 
  - +1 DM to melee combat (claws and fangs)
  - Enhanced jumping ability
  - Natural stealth in tall grass or jungle terrain

### Society
- **Clan System**: Society organized around clans (blood families)
  - Each clan has a chief (elder)
  - Clan loyalty supersedes all other loyalties
  - Inter-clan rivalry is common and often violent
- **Hierate Government**: 
  - The Hierate is ruled by the Hierarch, elected from among clan chiefs
  - The Hierarch serves for life
  - Council of Chiefs advises the Hierarch
- **Kuzu**: The sacred homeworld, site of the Hierarch's court
  - Interdicted by Aslan law — non-Aslan may not land
  - Political and religious center of the Hierate

### Culture
- **Honor Code**: Personal and clan honor are paramount
  - Duels of honor are common for disputes
  - Dishonor can lead to exile or death
- **Warrior Ethos**: Martial prowess is highly valued
  - Military service is expected of all able-bodied Aslan
  - Combat skills are taught from childhood
- **Territoriality**: Strong territorial instincts
  - Border disputes with Imperium are frequent
  - Expansionist tendencies
- **Social Structure**:
  - Chiefs (clan leaders)
  - Warriors (military class)
  - Hunters (scouts and rangers)
  - Workers (artisans and laborers)
  - Outcasts (exiled or dishonored)

### Character Generation (Aslan)

#### Block
- **ST**: 3D6+1 (claws and physical prowess)
- **DX**: 3D6 (enhanced reflexes)
- **ED**: 3D6
- **IN**: 3D6-1 (clan thinking over individual)
- **ML**: 3D6+1 (strong will)
- **BA**: 3D6

#### Skills
- **Combat**: Melee Weapons (claws), Firearms
- **Movement**: Running, Jumping, Stealth (in natural terrain)
- **Social**: Leadership (for chiefs), Intimidation
- **Survival**: Hunting, Tracking

#### Career Restrictions
- Aslan may serve in any career, but:
  - Naval careers are rare (territorial nature)
  - Military careers are preferred
  - Trade careers are uncommon (clan economy)

### The Aslan Hierate
- **Government**: Hierate (elective monarchy)
- **Population**: ~100 billion
- **Worlds**: ~1000
- **TL**: 12-14
- **Economy**: Resource-based, mining and agriculture
- **Military**: Large standing army, growing navy
- **Relations with Imperium**: Tense, frequent border conflicts
  - First Aslan Border War: -1120 to -1080
  - Second Aslan Border War: 700-720
  - Current status: Cold war, occasional skirmishes

### Aslan Traits (Game Mechanics)
- **Combat DM**: +1 melee (claws and fangs)
- **Social DM**: -1 with non-Aslan (xenophobia)
- **Navigation DM**: 0
- **Armor Bonus**: 0
- **Trade Bonus**: 0%
- **Special**: Enhanced night vision, acute smell, natural stealth in natural terrain

### Aslan Language
- **Primary**: Aslani (clicks, growls, roars)
- **Secondary**: Trade tongue (Imperial) for interstellar commerce
- **Writing**: Claw marks, pictographic

### Aslan Ships
- **Design Philosophy**: Heavy armor, heavy weapons, limited jump range
- **Common Types**: 
  - Assault ships (heavily armed, short-range)
  - Patrol vessels (border defense)
  - Transport ships (troop movement)
- **Notable**: Aslan ships are larger and more heavily armed than Imperial equivalents
