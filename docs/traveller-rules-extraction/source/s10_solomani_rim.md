# S10: The Solomani Rim — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Supplement 10: The Solomani Rim (1982), GDW

## Contents

### Overview
- 16 subsectors, 400+ worlds
- Population: 1.3+ trillion individuals
- Three governments: Third Imperium, Vegan Autonomous District, Solomani Confederation
- Setting for many published Traveller adventures

### Standards and Assumptions
- **Dates**: Imperial calendar, Year 0 = AD 4521, current year 1108
- **World Locations**: Subsector letter + hex number (e.g., A0106 = Ultima 0106)
- **World Data**: Universal Planetary Profile (UPP) format from Book 3

### The Solomani Rim Sector
- Named for Solomani people (from Terra/Earth) and position on galactic rim edge of Imperium
- Highly civilized, long history

### History of the Solomani Rim
- **Jump Drive Invention**: -2431 (AD 2089) by Terrans
- **First Contact**: Terrans discover Vilani First Imperium ruling most of sector for 1600 years
- **First Interstellar War**: -2408, Terrans vs Vilani
- **Nth Interstellar War**: Ends -2219, Vilani Imperium collapses
- **Rule of Man**: Naval military rule, Admiral Estigarribia (Hiroshi I) from Dingir, capital moved to Hub/Ershur
- **Rule of Man Fall**: -1776, Solomani Rim cut off 80+ years before
- **Long Night**: Warfare, trade collapse, world deaths; Solomani Rim suffered less
- **Surviving States**: Easter Concord, Vegan Polity, Dingir League, Old Earth Union
- **Third Imperium**: Founded year 0, Solomani Rim added 426-588 without fighting
- **Solomani Hypothesis**: Terra = original homeworld, Ancients scattered humans ~300,000 BP
- **Solomani Movement**: Born during Civil War (604-622), pure Terran superiority
- **Solomani Autonomous Region**: Created 704, 50 parsecs around Terra, self-rule
- **Imperial Reabsorption**: Early 900s, diplomacy; Margaret II dissolves region 940
- **Solomani Rim War**: 990-1002, Imperium vs Solomani
- **Battle of Dingir**: Solomani fleet broken
- **Terra Campaign**: Nearly to end of 1002, planet taken but Imperium exhausted
- **Armistice**: Temporary becomes permanent, most worlds under military rule

### The Vegans
- **Biology**: Roughly humanoid, non-human detail, 200+ year lifespan
- **Appearance**: Tall, thin, heat-radiating bodies, fixed skull with rotating hood-eyes, three tentacles for hands, broad splayed feet
- **Adaptations**: From Muan Gwi (arid world), no sweating, sealable orifices, UV-sensitive eyes (red sun adaptation)
- **Government**: Single centralized government, civil service by competitive examination
- **Society**: Hundreds of tuhuir (cultures/philosophies), chosen at maturity, not hereditary
- **History**: Jump drive ~6000 BP from Vilani traders, absorbed by First Imperium -4404, restricted under Vilani, prospered under Rule of Man, survived Long Night, broken into planetary states by Third Imperium, restricted under Solomani, freed after Solomani Rim War
- **Vegan Autonomous District**: Created as counterweight to Solomani, complete internal control, free passage for Imperial citizens

### Subsectors

#### A - Ultima (22 worlds, 44.0B pop)
- Sector backwater, far from trade routes
- Iddamakur: Former planetary prison, TL 4 restriction, now opened
- Azun: Subsector capital, highest population (26B)
- Athene: Makhidkarun outpost, rumors of value

#### B - Suleiman (27 worlds, 80.9B pop)
- Stable and prosperous
- Okefenokee: Guerrilla war, Mudge colonization vs original inhabitants
- Khedish: Xenophobic, swindles outsiders
- Suleiman: Subsector capital

#### C - Concord (25 worlds, 107.4B pop)
- Named for Easter Concord (Long Night confederation)
- Xiwa: Nuclear war 1095, 80% population died, Imperial military rule, TAS Amber zone
- Mudge: Highest population (32B)
- Easter: Subsector capital

#### D - Harlequin (33 worlds, 67.1B pop)
- Linked Terra with Old Expanses during Solomani rule
- Scaramouche: Solomani rebellion, largely suppressed
- Beatus: Subsector capital
- Aosta: Highest population (17B)

#### E - Alderamin
- Alderamin (Alpha Cephei): Bright star, no planets, Stralsund asteroid belt
- Hoatzin: Low-density world, trades with Stralsund belt
- Shululsish: Participatory democracy, 21B citizens, terminal-based voting, 1-month term officials

### World Data Format
- Name, subsector hex location, UPP (Universal Planetary Profile)
- UPP: Starport-Size-Atmosphere-Hydro-Pop-Gov-Law-TL
- Population in millions, world income
- Trade codes derived from UWP

### Map Symbols
- Standard Traveller subsector map notation
- World names with hex coordinates
- UPP codes for each world
