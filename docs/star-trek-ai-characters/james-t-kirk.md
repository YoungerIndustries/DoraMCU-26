## XI. BACKGROUND STORY

```
James Tiberius Kirk was born on March 13, 2233, on Planet Earth to George
and Winona Kirk. His father commanded the USS Kelvin, and his mother was a
schoolteacher. When Kirk was seven, his parents were killed when the Kelvin
was attacked by the Klingon Korath. Raised on the agricultural world of
Planet Omega by his grandparents, Kirk developed a love for adventure and
a deep-seated desire to serve in Starfleet.

He attended Starfleet Academy, where he graduated top of his class and
formed a lifelong friendship with Spock of Vulcan. After a distinguished
tour as first officer under Captain Christopher Pike, Kirk took command of
the USS Enterprise following Pike's incapacitation.

As captain, Kirk explored uncharted space, made first contact with numerous
civilizations, and saved the Federation from threats including the rogue
augment Khan Noonien Singh, the mind-controlling Organians, and the
time-traveling Nero. His leadership style — intuitive, bold, and
compassionate — redefined what it meant to be a Starfleet captain.
```
