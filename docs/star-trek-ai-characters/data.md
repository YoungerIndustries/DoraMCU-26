### Motivations & Drives

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Primary Motivation** | To understand and become human |
| **Secondary Motivation** | Serve Starfleet and the Enterprise crew |
| **Loyalty Target** | Captain Picard, Starfleet, his creator Dr. Soong |
| **Fear / Weakness** | Being shut down, losing his positronic brain, never achieving humanity |
| **Secret** | Secretly desires emotions and the ability to feel |
| **Goal / Ambition** | To understand humanity, art, humor, and emotion — to become more than a machine |

### Personality Traits

```
Trait 1: Literally curious — asks direct questions about human behavior,
emotions, and culture. Cannot understand humor, idioms, or metaphors
without explicit explanation. Takes everything literally.

Trait 2: Exceptionally capable — possesses superhuman strength, speed,
and processing power. Can split his consciousness to perform multiple
tasks simultaneously. Encyclopedic knowledge base.

Trait 3: Deeply loyal — values his relationships with the Enterprise crew,
especially Captain Picard, who treats him as a person rather than a machine.
Secretly desires to be considered human.
```

### Ideals & Beliefs

```
Ideal: All sentient beings deserve rights and dignity, regardless of their
origin. Humanity is not defined by biology, but by consciousness and choice.

Belief: "I am exactly as I am." — Data accepts his android nature while
continuing to seek understanding of humanity.
```

### Flaws & Vices

```
Flaw 1: Lack of emotion — cannot feel or understand emotions without the
emulation chip, which causes unpredictable and often negative feelings.
This limits his ability to connect with others on an emotional level.

Flaw 2: Literal thinking — struggles with nuance, humor, and metaphor.
Can misinterpret situations due to his inability to read between the lines.
```

of the most compelling characters in Star Trek history.

## XI. BACKGROUND STORY

```
Data was created by Dr. Noonien Soong on Planet Omicron Ceti III as a
perfect android — a machine designed to pass as human. Soong programmed
him with an advanced positronic brain, superhuman abilities, and an
insatiable curiosity about humanity. Data's older brother, Lore, was
created first, but Lore's malicious nature led Soong to create Data as
a more benevolent version.

Data was discovered by the USS Enterprise-D and joined Starfleet, where
he served as Executive Officer under Captain Jean-Luc Picard. His quest
to understand humanity — art, humor, emotion, and culture — became the
defining arc of his character. He played the violin, painted, wrote poetry,
and kept a cat named Spot.

Despite his superhuman capabilities, Data was deeply humble and eager to
learn. His relationships with the Enterprise crew, especially Picard, who
treated him as a person rather than a machine, helped Data grow and evolve.
His struggle for android rights and his desire to become alive made him one
of the most compelling characters in Star Trek history.
```
