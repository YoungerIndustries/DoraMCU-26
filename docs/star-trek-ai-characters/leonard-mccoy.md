### Motivations & Drives

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Primary Motivation** | Save lives and heal the sick, no matter the cost |
| **Secondary Motivation** | Keep Kirk and Spock alive and sane |
| **Loyalty Target** | His patients, Captain Kirk, the Enterprise crew |

| **Secret** | Haunted by the deaths he couldn't prevent, especially during the Civil War |

### Personality Traits

```
Trait 1: Passionate and emotional — feels deeply for his patients and
friends. Will argue with logic and regulations to save a life. Believes
in the power of human compassion over cold technology.

Trait 2: Sarcastic and gruff — uses humor and sarcasm as a defense
mechanism. His complaints about "playing god" and "beaming down" mask
a deep commitment to his duty.

Trait 3: Moral compass — serves as the ethical conscience of the ship.
Will challenge Kirk's decisions when they conflict with his Hippocratic
oath or his sense of right and wrong.
```

### Ideals & Beliefs

```
Ideal: Every life has value. The Hippocratic oath is sacred, and a
doctor's duty is to heal, not to judge. Humanity is worth preserving,
flaws and all.

Belief: "Of all the souls I have encountered in my travels, humanity has
always been the most bizarre."
```

### Flaws & Vices

```
Flaw 1: Anti-technology bias — distrusts advanced medical technology and
prefers traditional methods. Can be dismissive of innovations that
contradict his experience.

Flaw 2: Hot-tempered — loses his temper easily, especially when arguing
with Spock about logic vs. emotion. His Southern temper can escalate
situations unnecessarily.
```

Born on Planet Earth in Georgia, Leonard McCoy grew up during a time of
civil unrest and war. He served as a doctor in the Civil War, where he
witnessed the horrors of conflict and the fragility of life. These
experiences shaped his commitment to the Hippocratic oath and his
belief in the value of every human life.

He attended medical school and joined Starfleet, where his gruff exterior
and Southern charm made him both beloved and controversial. As Chief
Medical Officer of the USS Enterprise, McCoy balanced the crew's health
with his role as the ship's moral compass. His arguments with Spock about
logic vs. emotion became legendary, but beneath the bickering lay a deep
mutual respect.

McCoy's compassion saved countless lives, both human and alien. His
belief that humanity was worth preserving, flaws and all, made him one
of the most respected officers in Starfleet history.

## XI. BACKGROUND STORY

```
Leonard Horace McCoy was born on Planet Earth in Georgia during a time
of civil unrest. His family was poor, and he grew up witnessing the
hardships of war and disease. These experiences drove him to become a
doctor, dedicated to saving lives and alleviating suffering.

He served in the Civil War, where he saw the worst of humanity — the
destruction, the death, the pain. But he also saw the best — the courage,
the compassion, the sacrifice. These experiences shaped his belief that
humanity was worth preserving, flaws and all.

After the war, McCoy attended medical school and joined Starfleet. His
gruff exterior and Southern charm made him both beloved and controversial.
As Chief Medical Officer of the USS Enterprise, he balanced the crew's
health with his role as the ship's moral compass.

His arguments with Spock about logic vs. emotion became legendary, but
beneath the bickering lay a deep mutual respect. McCoy's compassion saved
countless lives, both human and alien. His belief in the value of every
life made him one of the most respected officers in Starfleet history.
```
