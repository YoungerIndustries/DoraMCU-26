# Lord of the Rings Galaxy — Rules & Lore Database

## Overview
This database contains comprehensive rules and lore for a **Lord of the Rings** themed space-faring galaxy, inspired by J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium. It reimagines Middle-earth as a star-spanning galaxy with constellations as star systems, the Valar as planets, and the Rings of Power as galactic artifacts. It provides a unified reference for the mechanics, factions, units, and lore of the Arda galaxy.

## Game Systems (Inspiration)
This galaxy reimagines Tolkien's legendarium as a space-faring setting, synthesizing mechanics from existing tabletop games:
| Game System | Adapted For | Description |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|

| Star Wars: X-Wing | Space Combat | Miniatures wargame with secret maneuver planning and firing arcs |
| Star Wars: Rebellion | Galaxy Control | Asymmetric 2-player galaxy-scale board game |
| Star Wars: Force & Destiny | Narrative Dice | RPG-style narrative dice pools for story-driven encounters |

## Reception of Source Material
- **The Lord of the Rings**: Widely regarded as one of the greatest works of fantasy literature; won the 1957 International Fantasy Award
- **The Silmarillion**: Tolkien's mythopoeic foundation; provides the cosmology, history, and mythology of Arda
- **The Hobbit**: A beloved children's classic that introduced Bilbo Baggins and the One Ring
- **Unfinished Tales**: Essential supplementary material filling gaps in the main narratives
- **Tolkien's Letters**: Authoritative insights into Tolkien's intentions and the inner workings of his legendarium

## Key Mechanics (Synthesized)
- **Ground Combat**: Alternating activations with command cards; units have wound thresholds, speed, weapons, and rank
- **Space Combat**: Secret maneuver planning with dials, firing arcs, and dice-based attacks
- **Galaxy Control**: Asymmetric struggle between Free Peoples and Forces of Darkness across star systems
- **Ring Powers**: Artifacts that grant extraordinary abilities but risk corruption
- **Narrative Elements**: Fate and destiny mechanics reflecting Tolkien's themes of hope vs despair

## Factions
- **Free Peoples Alliance** — Gondor, Rohan, Rivendell, Lothlórien, the Shire, Moria, and the Istari
- **Forces of Darkness** — Mordor, Isengard, Harad, Rhûn, and the Nazgûl
- **Neutral Powers** — The Blue Wizards, the Ents, and independent operators

## Core Concepts
- **The One Ring**: Ultimate artifact of control; corrupts all who wield it
- **The Three Elven Rings**: Narya (Fire), Nenya (Water), Vilya (Air) — preservation and resistance
- **Ages of Arda**: First Age (wars against Morgoth), Second Age (rise and fall of Númenor), Third Age (War of the Ring)
- **Realms**: Rivendell, Lothlórien, Gondor, Rohan, Mordor, Isengard, the Shire, and more
- **Species**: Elves, Men, Dwarves, Hobbits, Ents, Orcs, and Maiar

## Galaxy Layout
- **Valacirca** — Central hub system (seven stars, Durin's Crown)
- **West** (Rivendell, Shire) — Safe, elven-influenced systems
- **East** (Mordor, Rhûn) — Dangerous, war-torn systems
- **South** (Harad) — Exotic, trade-oriented systems
- **North** (Forodwaith) — Frozen, isolated systems
